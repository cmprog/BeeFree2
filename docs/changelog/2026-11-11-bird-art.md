# Bird art (2026-11-11)

## Summary

`src/` is reorganized into feature folders, and the birds have new art drawn in code: cleaner,
animated, and with a palette per bird type.

## What changed for players

- New bird art: the same cartoon birds, cleaner, flapping their wings and blinking.
- Each bird type has its own colors, including a belly, wing and tail color. Thing 1 to 4 can now
  be told apart by their tails.
- Bird health bars sit a little higher, above the raised wings.

## Under the hood

- `src/` now has feature folders: `core/`, `player/`, `birds/`, `levels/`, `hats/`, `entities/`,
  `menus/` (with `menus/dev/` for localhost-only tools). `game.js` and `settings.js` stay at the top.
  Files were renamed where the folder made the old prefix redundant, e.g. `menu-shop.js` →
  `menus/shop.js`, `hat-adjustments.js` → `hats/adjustments.js`. Two renames for clarity:
  `owl.js` → `entities/sammy.js` and `entities.js` → `entities/hud.js`. The layout is described in
  `CLAUDE.md`.
- The module-loading test now walks `src/` subfolders.
- New bird art drawn in code (`birds/art.js`): cleaner cartoon birds with a flapping animation and
  blinking. Each bird type has its own palette (`withStyle`, replacing `withColors`), with a belly,
  wing and tail color as well as body and head. Thing 1 to 4 now differ by tail color. Sprite sheets
  are drawn once at startup (`initBirdArt()`), and a bird draws one sprite instead of five part
  objects. The old part classes are gone, and `img/bird.png` moved to `img/originals/` for reference.
- The images to load are listed by name in `core/sprites.js` (`IMAGES`), and textures are looked up
  by name instead of by their position in the list.
- Settings > Debug > Bird Preview (localhost) shows every bird type animated.

## Decisions

- Folders are by feature, not by kind of file. No `index.js` re-export files, to avoid import
  cycles.
- `attributes.js` lives in `player/`.
- The old bird art is kept in `img/originals/`, not deleted.
- The legs were angled back to be closer to the original; further tweaks to the art can wait.

## Follow-ups

- Bird hats looked right in a quick check, but are worth a pass in Hat Tuning.
