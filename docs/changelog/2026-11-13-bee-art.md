# Bee art (2026-11-13)

## Summary

The bee is redrawn in code like the birds: the same cartoon bee, cleaner, with a fast wing buzz and
blinking.

## What changed for players

- New bee art, close to the old look: a slightly warmer yellow, clean outlines, a rosy cheek.
- The wings buzz fast (24 frames a second instead of 4), with a faint blur, and the bee blinks.
- The hitbox is exactly the same 2 x 2 as before, and hats sit on the head as before.
- The wardrobe preview shows the new bee.

## Under the hood

- `entities/bee-art.js` draws the bee into a sprite sheet at startup (`initBeeArt()`). The game, the
  wardrobe preview and Hat Tuning all draw from it.
- The helpers shared by bird and bee art moved to `core/art.js`: outlined shapes, `shade`,
  `FrameSheet` (a sprite sheet with eyes-open and eyes-closed rows) and `FlapBlinkAnimation`. The
  birds use them too; their art is unchanged.
- `img/bee.png` moved to `img/originals/` and is no longer loaded.
- Bird Preview is now Art Preview, with the bee as its first entry.
- New tests: the bee's hitbox stays 2 x 2, and the wings cycle through every frame.

## Decisions

- The BEE hat anchor is unchanged: the new head was drawn to sit under it.
- The old bee art is kept in `img/originals/` so it can be restored.
